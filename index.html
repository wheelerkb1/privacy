Privacy policy

This app is a private tool used only by its owner to read and organise the owner's own email, forms and spreadsheets. It is not offered to the public.

Data it reads stays on the owner's own computers and Google account. It is not sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.

The owner can remove the app's access at any time from the Google account's security settings.

Questions: contact the owner of the Google account that uses this app.
